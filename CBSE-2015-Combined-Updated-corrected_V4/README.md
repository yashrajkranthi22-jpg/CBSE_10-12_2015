Combined CBSE Mathematics 2015 — Classes 10 and 12

Canonical combined build. Section 1 is Class 10 and Section 2 is Class 12. Within each section, subsection 1 is Formulae and subsection 2 is Problems. Question and formula indices are generated through enumerate counters; equations, figures and tables inherit the active list index.

The two style files are byte-for-byte copies of `gadepall/ee1010-2026/EE26BTECH11202`. The original class directories are retained as figure/code asset stores and as historical standalone PDF snapshots.

Build from this directory with `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -file-line-error main.tex`.
